Let's Go Ice Fishing

  • Godot
  • GDScript
My final year project for Year 1 of my CS4G degree. Made in a multidiciplinary team. I was the technical and programming lead.
  • Made over 4 weeks using Agile methodologies. I took lead in most of our scrums and standups.
  • I was the only dedicated programeer. One of the designers learnt to program under my supervision and was a great asset.
  • I was solely responsible for the fishing part of the game as well as some of the overworld. I also created the interfaces and events to link all part of the game together.
  • Prior to this project none of the team had used Godot before. We initially started on Unreal Engine, but quicly realised that it wasn't the right engine for our pixel art 2D game. Whilst Unity was an option and most of the team had some experience with it, that experience was not positive. Godot was an exellent engine, as well as easy to pick up. GDScript, with it's Python-like syntax, was also simple and permisive enough to pick up and use for a short project like this; I did insist that static typing was used for any game code to aid development.
